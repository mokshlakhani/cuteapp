Privacy policy

soft spot · last updated 9 November 2026

In short: soft spot collects nothing about you. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no data leaves your device.

What the app keeps

To remember where you left off, soft spot saves a few small things in the app's own storage on your device:

  • your sound, music and haptics settings;
  • which how-to-play hints you have already seen;
  • your best scores and progress in each toy.

This never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and it shares nothing with third parties.
  • It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • It does not need an internet connection to play.

Children

soft spot is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

The website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical details (such as IP addresses) when pages are served; see GitHub's privacy statement. The website itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub, or see the support page.
