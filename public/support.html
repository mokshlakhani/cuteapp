How do I play a toy?

Open any toy and tap the ? in its top bar for a short how-to-play card.

There's no sound

Check that your device isn't on silent and its volume is up, then tap the sound button at the top left of the home screen to turn sound and music on.

How do I reset my scores?

Your progress is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app starts everything fresh.

Something's not working

Sorry about that! Please open an issue on GitHub and tell us your device, its iOS version and what happened. We read every one.

Privacy
