    how it feels

    made for unwinding

    Small, warm and quiet on purpose — the toy is the star, and everything else stays soft.

    • nothing to fail

      No timers, coins or lives. Every ending — a burst, a spill, a toppled tower — tidies itself up and starts again gently.

    • every sound in one key

      No audio files: each pluck, pop and glug is made as you play and tuned to one soft scale, so even mashing sounds nice. Slow music and a crackling fire sit underneath.

    • everything is alive

      Toys breathe, blink and watch your finger. Squeeze one and it squints; push it too far and it gets a little nervous.

    • you can feel it

      On a phone, every band, slice and landing has its own small tap — never a buzz.

    • dims at bedtime

      When your device is in dark mode the whole room settles into a deeper dusk, for playing before sleep.

    • yours, on this device

      Your bests stay on your device. No account, no tracking, and once it has loaded it plays offline.
